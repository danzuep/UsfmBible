---
title: Privacy policy
---

USFM Bible is a free, open source Bible reader for Android. [Home page](https://danzuep.github.io/UsfmBible/).

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting, and the developer receives no data from it.

## What stays on your device

Your notes, bookmarks, highlights, reading progress, settings and downloaded translations are stored only on your device. Backups you create are files you choose where to save.

## Network use

The app connects to the internet only to list and download translations from [eBible.org](https://ebible.org), or from download sites you add yourself, and to check installed translations for updates. Those servers can see your IP address and the files you request, under their own policies. Reading needs no connection once a translation is installed.

## Permissions

- Internet and network state: download translations and check for updates.
- Notifications: show download and read-aloud playback status.

Read-aloud uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device, which has its own privacy policy.

## Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Contact

Questions or requests: [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/danzuep/UsfmBible/issues/new).
